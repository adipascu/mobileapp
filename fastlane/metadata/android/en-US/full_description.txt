The Pebble app is a free and open-source Android companion for Pebble and
compatible Core Devices smartwatches. Its core Bluetooth features work without
an account or network service.

Features include:

* Bluetooth pairing, reconnection, and watch management
* Phone notifications, calls, contacts, and calendar integration
* Local watch settings, health data, screenshots, logs, and developer tools
* Optional watchface and watch-app browsing and installation
* Optional weather, language-pack, voice, and firmware services

Network and external software

Weather is off by default. Enabling it sends coordinates for configured
locations to weather-api.repebble.com; using Current Location also requires the
Android location permission. The separate legacy watch-app weather proxy is
also off by default; enabling it may send coordinates to
weather-api.repebble.com or api.openweathermap.org.

The optional app catalog, firmware, language packs, voice features, and web
proxies use third-party Core/Rebble or community services. Software installed
on a watch is not built or reviewed by F-Droid and may have a different
license. The app asks before downloading third-party watch apps or firmware
and explains that they bypass F-Droid's checks. Broad external file/content
package handlers are disabled in this build.

F-Droid build

This build excludes Firebase and Google sign-in, Crashlytics, Mixpanel, Google
Play updates, Health Connect, proprietary Index/Haversine support, Krisp, and
Cactus native speech models. Weather and optional watch-diagnostic uploads
default off, and firmware checks require an informed user action. Index 01 ring
features and cloud-backed account synchronization are unavailable.

Permissions

Bluetooth and nearby-device permissions connect to watches. Optional features
may request notification-listener, contacts, calendar, phone/call-log, or
location access; they can be left disabled when those integrations are not
wanted.

Another Pebble companion app may conflict with this app's Bluetooth and
PebbleKit compatibility services. Disable or uninstall the other companion if
connections are unreliable.
